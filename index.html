Our Story

A neighborhood café built on warmth, quality, and the simple ritual of a perfect cup.

Roasted with intention

Ember & Cream began as a dream to bring specialty coffee culture to Colombo — without losing the comfort of your favorite local spot. We source beans from trusted farms, roast in small batches, and serve each drink at its best.

Our space is designed for conversation, reading, and quiet mornings alike. Pastries arrive fresh each day from local bakers who share our obsession with quality ingredients.
